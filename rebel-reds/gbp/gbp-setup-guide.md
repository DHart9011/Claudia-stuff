## STEP 1: CHECK IF A LISTING ALREADY EXISTS

Before creating anything, check whether Google already has a listing for the business.

1. Go to **google.com** and search: `Rebel Red's Gyros Lamesa TX`
2. Look at the right-side panel (Knowledge Panel). If a listing appears, it may already exist — partially or fully claimed.
3. Also search: `gyroplane dealer Lamesa TX` and `gyroplane flight school Lamesa TX` to check for duplicates under different names.

**If a listing appears and says "Claim this business":**
- Click that link and follow Google's verification steps (phone call or postcard to 1744 County Rd N).

**If no listing appears:**
- Proceed to Step 2 to create one from scratch.

## STEP 2: CREATE OR ACCESS YOUR GBP LISTING

1. Go to **business.google.com**
2. Sign in with your Google account (use matt@rebelredsgyros.com if possible — keep business accounts tied to your business email)
3. Click **"Add your business to Google"** or **"Manage now"** if a listing was found
4. Enter business name: `Rebel Red's Gyros`
5. When prompted for a category, type `Aircraft Dealer` and select it (see Step 3 for full category setup)
6. Enter your address: `1744 County Rd N, Lamesa, TX 79331`
7. Choose whether customers visit your location — select **Yes**
8. Add phone number: `432-517-0693`
9. Add website: `rebelredsgyros.com`
10. Click **Continue** and proceed through the verification flow

**Verification:** Google will likely verify by phone call or postcard. Choose phone call if available — it's faster. The postcard can take 5-14 days.

---

## STEP 3: SET BUSINESS CATEGORIES

Categories help Google decide when to show your listing. Set these exactly.

**Primary Category:** `Aircraft Dealer`

**Secondary Categories (add both):**
- `Flight School`
- `Aviation Service`

**How to add categories:**
1. In your GBP dashboard, click **Edit profile**
2. Scroll to "Business category"
3. The first category you entered during setup is your Primary — confirm it's `Aircraft Dealer`
4. Click **"Add another category"** and add `Flight School`
5. Add again: `Aviation Service`
6. Save

## STEP 8: Q&A SEEDS

Post these yourself in the GBP Q&A section before customers ask. This pre-populates common questions and helps your listing rank for long-tail searches.

**How to post:** On your GBP listing (viewed as a customer on Google Maps), scroll to the Q&A section. Click "Ask a question" and type the question yourself, then switch to your business account to answer it.

**Q1:**
**Q:** Do I need a pilot's license to take an intro flight?
**A:** No — no license, no experience, nothing. Our intro flight is designed for first-timers. Matt will walk you through everything on the ground before you ever get in the aircraft. You just need to show up. Book at rebelredsgyros.com or call 432-517-0693.

---

**Q2:**
**Q:** How much does a gyroplane cost?
**A:** Entry-level Magni models start in the $40,000-$60,000 range for used aircraft. New Magni gyroplanes vary by model — the best way to get accurate pricing is to call Matt directly at 432-517-0693. We sell the M16, M22, M24, and M26. No pressure, just straight answers.

---

**Q3:**
**Q:** Is a gyroplane safe?
**A:** Gyroplanes have an excellent safety record when properly flown. The key feature is autorotation — the rotor keeps spinning freely even without engine power, which means the aircraft glides down safely if the engine ever stops. Modern Magni gyroplanes with Rotax engines are very reliable. Proper training is the biggest safety factor. Call us to talk it through.

---

**Q4:**
**Q:** Where are you located and do I need to drive to the airport?
**A:** We're at 1744 County Rd N, Lamesa TX 79331 — not a major commercial airport, so come directly to our address. Look for the red hangar. If you need directions, call Matt at 432-517-0693 and he'll get you sorted.

---

**Q5:**
**Q:** How long does the intro flight take?
**A:** Plan for about 1.5 to 2 hours total — ground briefing, pre-flight walkthrough, the flight itself, and a debrief after. Actual air time depends on conditions and your comfort level. We don't rush it.
