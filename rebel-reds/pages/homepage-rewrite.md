## MOBILE CONSIDERATIONS

### Sections That Stack Vertically on Mobile (375px)
- **Section 3 (Intro Flight Block):** Image above text, text block full-width below, CTA full-width at bottom.
- **Section 4 (Model Grid):** Single column — cards stack one per row. Maintain card min-height so content doesn't collapse.
- **Section 5 (Trust Block):** Photo above text/credentials. Credentials list full-width below.
- **Section 7 (Services):** Tiles stack vertically. Each tile full-width.
- **Section 8 (Footer CTA):** CTAs stack — "Call Matt" above "Book Online."

### CTA Visibility on 375px Screen
- **Primary CTA (Book Your Intro Flight):** Must be visible on first scroll stop — keep hero height no taller than 85vh on mobile so CTA is visible or just below the fold. Use full-width button (100% width, 48px min height) for easy tap.
- **Sticky CTA Bar (Optional):** Consider a thin sticky bar at the bottom of mobile viewport with "Book: 432-517-0693 | Book Online" that appears after user scrolls past the hero. Squarespace supports this via custom code block.
- **Phone CTA:** Use `<a href="tel:4325170693">` so it triggers native dialer on mobile.

### Image Sizing Recommendations for Squarespace
- **Hero background video/image:** 1920x1080px minimum. Squarespace will compress — upload at full resolution. For video: MP4, H.264, under 30MB.
- **Intro Flight section image:** 800x1000px (portrait) or 1000x800px (landscape). Keep subject-critical content away from edges for mobile crop.
- **Trust section photo (Matt & Pam):** 900x600px minimum. Squarespace fluid grid will crop — keep faces in center frame.
- **Model cards:** 600x400px per card. Consistent dimensions across all 4 cards for visual alignment.
- **Testimonial section:** No images needed unless you add customer photos (optional).
- **All images:** Export as JPG at 85% quality. Squarespace has a 20MB image limit; most aircraft photos at proper resolution will be well under that.
