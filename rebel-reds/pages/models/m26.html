Aircraft Overview

Further. Faster. Better.

The Magni M26 is the flagship of the lineup — an enclosed side-by-side two-seater that takes everything the M24 offers and pushes it further. With a higher-output engine option (Rotax 914 or 915) and performance figures to match, the M26 is the answer when "good enough" isn't good enough. If you're planning long cross-country flights, need more climb capability for density altitude conditions, or simply want the highest-performing production gyroplane Magni builds, this is the aircraft.

The M26 shares the enclosed cockpit comfort of the M24 — doors, side-by-side seating, weather protection — but it adds meaningful performance headroom. That margin matters in West Texas summer heat, where density altitude can rob a naturally aspirated engine of real performance. The M26's turbocharged option addresses that directly. You get the same gyroplane safety characteristics — no stall, no accelerated stall, minimal footprint — with the power reserve of a more capable powerplant.

This is a pilot's aircraft. Not because it's difficult — Magni's gyroplanes are known for tractable handling — but because the buyer who chooses the M26 has usually flown a lot, knows what they want, and has decided not to compromise on capability.

"The M26 is for the pilot who's done their research, knows what a gyroplane can do, and wants the best one built. It's a serious aircraft for serious pilots."

— Matt Snell, CFI / MagniGyro Factory Certified

Financing

Investing in the Best

The M26 represents the top of the Magni lineup, and buyers considering it are typically experienced pilots with a clear picture of how they'll use the aircraft. Sport aviation financing has matured considerably — lenders who specialize in light sport and experimental aircraft can offer terms comparable to general aviation aircraft loans, and at M26 price points, financing structures that work for most serious buyers are accessible. Down payments typically range from 15–20%, with repayment periods up to 15 years for well-qualified buyers.

Matt works with M26 buyers differently than first-time gyroplane purchasers. If you're at the point where you're evaluating the M26, you've probably already done significant research.
