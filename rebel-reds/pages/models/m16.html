Aircraft Overview

Built to Teach. Built to Feel.

The Magni M16 is the backbone of gyroplane instruction worldwide — and it's the aircraft Matt Snell uses to take students from their very first flight to Sport Pilot certification. The tandem two-seat layout puts instructor and student in line, exactly like the classic training configuration that maximizes the instructor's visibility and control authority during dual instruction.

What sets the M16 apart from the rest of the Magni lineup is its open cockpit. There's no windshield between you and the sky. You feel the air, you feel the rotor, and you develop the kind of intuitive feel for the aircraft that's hard to build in an enclosed cabin. If you're coming for training — or if you simply want the most tactile flying experience available — the M16 delivers.

Compared to the M22 (solo only) and the M24/M26 (enclosed), the M16 occupies the sweet spot: two seats for shared adventure or instruction, and the raw open-air experience that makes gyroplane flying so distinctive.

"The M16 is where pilots are made. There's nothing like sitting in open air, hands on the controls, with nothing above you but rotor blades and Texas sky."

— Matt Snell, CFI / MagniGyro Factory Certified
