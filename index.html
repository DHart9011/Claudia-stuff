Shape + color = meaning, even when a sign is damaged

Even when a sign is dirty or damaged, you should recognize it from its shape and color. Here's the full system from the Oklahoma manual.

Color cheat sheet

Memorize these — guaranteed test material
  • RED — Stop or "Not Allowed"
  • ORANGE — Construction / maintenance / work zone
  • YELLOW — General warning (hazard ahead)
  • FLUORESCENT YELLOW-GREEN — Schools, pedestrians, bicycles
  • WHITE/BLACK — Regulations & instructions
  • GREEN — Guide / direction signs
  • BLUE — Driver services (gas, food, lodging, hospitals)
  • BROWN — Recreation, cultural, historical
